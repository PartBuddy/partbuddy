## What's new

- **Names and logos on round parts:** text and SVG logos now go on a round wall such as a cup or a handle and wrap round it, through the block form or the assistant. A ring in a logo keeps its hole. One text or logo per wall; logos in colours stay on flat faces.
- **Repair closes more parts:** an imported part with a whole face missing is now patched and closed where SolidCraft's own repair gives up. Most such holes on flat faces close; a missing curved face still stays open.
- **Large STL files:** meshes over 20,000 triangles are thinned to that before they are turned into faces, and the notice says so. Before, SolidCraft refused anything over 50,000.
- **Screw holes that miss:** the print check warns when holes in two touching parts nearly line up, so a screw would not pass through both.
- **Steadier with big designs:** a large part no longer cuts PartBuddy off from SolidCraft.
- **SolidCraft 0.49.0:** recommended now; 0.48 still works.
