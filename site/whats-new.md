## What's new

- **Repair open parts:** a STEP or IGES part the print check finds open now offers "Repair" in the structure tree. SolidCraft sews its gaps and patches its holes in one step you can undo.
- **Thin pins:** a new hint in the print check shows pins and posts under 3 mm, which break off easily. Turn it off in the print settings like the other hints.
- **A more honest light:** it names hidden bodies ("Ready to print, 1 hidden"), waits while standard parts are still being built, and stays off green while a measure failed.
- **Fits that follow your printer:** a snap closure's lip gets the material's slide fit, and guides and screw lids need a wider gap on nozzles over 0.4 mm, so nothing fuses on an 0.8 mm nozzle.
- **Nut pockets that open downwards** keep one layer over the screw hole, so their roof prints as a bridge. Push it through with the screw after printing.
- **Stronger snap and latch arms:** they are sized to hold even when the part prints standing.
- **Imports:** an STL shows its size right after import, and "Convert to faces" says how much the volume changed.
- **Assistant:** it can no longer switch off a failing feature, and it hears when a resized screw grew into its hole.
- **SolidCraft 0.45.0** is the recommended version; PartBuddy no longer runs one older than its minimum.
